Usage Rules
Clear space
Keep open space around the mark equal to the height of the fermata arc on every side. Nothing else sits inside that margin.
Minimum size
Mark-only: 32px tall minimum. Full lockup: 120px wide minimum, so the subline stays legible.
Don’t
Don’t stretch or rotate the mark, recolor it outside the palette, or place it on a busy photo without the single-color version.

Rest & Reset

Eight marks built from a second round of notes. The palette, typography and usage rules carry over from V1.

A curvier, anatomical rest
The whole rest is redrawn as a vertebra silhouette: the bar is the transverse processes, the hanging block is the vertebral body.
Sunset from the beach
The fermata arc is the sky and its dot is the setting sun. The whole rest is the sun’s reflection under the horizon, and the ripples taper like a spine.
“Am I doing too much?”
Concepts 1, 3, 5, 6 and 8 each use one idea. Concepts 2 and 4 combine ideas; 2, 4 and 7 get a dimensional finish.
