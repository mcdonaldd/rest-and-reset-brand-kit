Usage Rules
Clear space
Keep open space around the mark equal to the height of the fermata arc on every side. Nothing else sits inside that margin.
Minimum size
Mark-only: 32px tall minimum. Full lockup: 120px wide minimum, so the subline stays legible.
Don’t
Don’t stretch or rotate the mark, recolor it outside the palette, or place it on a busy photo without the single-color version.
