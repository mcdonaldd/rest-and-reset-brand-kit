A Thoughtful Approach to Care

At Rest & Reset, care begins with understanding.

The upper cervical spine — the region closest to the brain and brainstem — plays a significant role in neurological regulation. Restriction, altered proprioception, or imbalance in this region can contribute to patterns that extend far beyond the neck.
