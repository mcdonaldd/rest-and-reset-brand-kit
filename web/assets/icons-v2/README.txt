Rest & Reset — V2 SVG assets
Eight directions from Dr. Wilson's feedback round. Each concept folder:
  mark-color.svg      the concept's real finish (gradient + shadow where the direction calls for one)
  mark-teal/black/white/cream.svg   flat single-color versions
  avatar-circle.svg   social avatar
  lockup-stacked.svg / lockup-horizontal.svg   mark + wordmark

Lockups use live text (Lora, Piazzolla, Archivo — free on Google Fonts).
Before sending to a printer or sign shop, open in Illustrator/Figma/Inkscape and convert text to outlines.
Colors: Deep Teal #1F4A48 · Teal Tint #C9DEDC · Warm Cream #E8DCC8 · Clay #B5764A · Ink #152E2D
